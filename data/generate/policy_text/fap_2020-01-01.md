# Halcyon Bay Community Hospital
## Financial Assistance Policy

Version effective January 1, 2020. Approved by the Board of Directors. Replaced by the version effective July 1, 2025.

### Purpose

Halcyon Bay Community Hospital is a nonprofit hospital. We provide free or discounted care to patients who cannot afford to pay for the emergency or medically necessary care they receive from us. This policy explains who qualifies, how to apply and what we do before any balance is sent to collections.

### Who qualifies

Eligibility is based on yearly household income compared with the federal poverty guidelines for the year of the decision.

- Household income at or below 200% of the poverty guidelines: **free care**. The full patient balance is written off.
- Household income above 200% and up to 400%: **discounted care**. The patient pays 30% of the billed amount (the patient balance after insurance), and the rest is written off.
- Household income above 400%: not eligible for free or discounted care. The patient may pay the balance on an interest-free payment plan.

Patients enrolled in Medi-Cal, and patients who are homeless, receive free care without income documents.

### Assets

Savings and other monetary assets are counted. A household with more than $10,000 in savings, checking or other monetary assets does not qualify, whatever its income. The application asks for the total.

### How to apply

Ask any registration desk or the Patient Financial Services office for an application, or call the number on your statement. Return the completed application with your **two most recent pay stubs** for every working member of the household. Pay stubs are required to verify income. Applications without pay stubs are incomplete. We will ask once for what is missing and close an application that is still incomplete after 30 days.

Applications are accepted for **240 days after the first bill** for the care. Applications received later are not considered.

### What an approval covers

An approval covers the accounts listed on the application and every visit in the 12 months from the decision date.

### Payment plans

Payment plans are interest-free. The balance is split into 12 equal monthly payments rounded up to the dollar, and no payment is less than $25 a month.

We only end a payment plan after the patient has missed payments for 90 days in a row, and only after we try to call and send a written notice offering to set up a new plan. Until then the account stays out of collections.

### Billing and collections

We send a first bill after discharge and a monthly statement while a balance is owed. Before any extraordinary collection action (reporting to a credit bureau, a lawsuit, a lien or selling the debt) we wait at least 120 days from the first bill and mail a written notice at least 30 days before the action. We do not file a lawsuit or sell a debt before 180 days from the first bill. Unpaid balances may be placed with our collection agency, Golden Coast Recovery. After 180 days from the first bill the agency may report an unpaid balance to the credit bureaus.

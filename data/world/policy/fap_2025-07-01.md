# Halcyon Bay Community Hospital
## Financial Assistance Policy

Version effective July 1, 2025. Approved by the Board of Directors. Replaces the version effective January 1, 2020.

### Purpose

Halcyon Bay Community Hospital is a nonprofit hospital. We provide free or discounted care to patients who cannot afford to pay for the emergency or medically necessary care they receive from us. This policy explains who qualifies, how to apply and what we do before any balance is sent to collections.

### Who qualifies

Eligibility is based on yearly household income compared with the federal poverty guidelines for the year of the decision.

- Household income at or below 200% of the poverty guidelines: **free care**. The full patient balance is written off.
- Household income above 200% and up to 400%: **discounted care**. The patient pays 30% of the billed amount (the patient balance after insurance), and the rest is written off.
- Household income above 400%: not eligible for free or discounted care. The patient may pay the balance on an interest-free payment plan.

Patients enrolled in Medi-Cal, and patients who are homeless, receive free care without income documents.

Savings and other monetary assets are not considered.

### How to apply

Ask any registration desk or the Patient Financial Services office for an application, or call the number on your statement. Return the completed application with proof of household income. Recent pay stubs are the easiest proof, but we also accept any of the following when there are no pay stubs:

- the most recent federal tax return,
- a letter from the employer stating the pay,
- a benefit letter (for example unemployment, Social Security or disability),
- a signed self-attestation of income, when nothing else exists.

There is no deadline. We accept an application at any time while a balance is owed, and after it is paid.

### What an approval covers

An approval covers the accounts listed on the application and every visit in the 12 months from the decision date.

### Payment plans

Payment plans are interest-free. The balance is split into 12 equal monthly payments rounded up to the dollar, and no payment is less than $25 a month.

### Billing and collections

We send a first bill after discharge and a monthly statement while a balance is owed. Before any extraordinary collection action (a lawsuit, a lien or selling the debt) we wait at least 120 days from the first bill, check whether the patient qualifies for financial assistance, and mail a written notice at least 30 days before the action. We do not file a lawsuit or sell a debt before 180 days from the first bill. Unpaid balances may be placed with our collection agency, Golden Coast Recovery. Neither the hospital nor the agency ever reports a patient balance to a credit bureau.
